Privacy policy

KIRA OS has no accounts and collects as little as possible.

Stored in your browser

Your agents, chats, memories, knowledge documents and settings are saved in your own browser (localStorage, plus IndexedDB for the docs). They are not uploaded to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Sent when you chat

When you send a message, the conversation needed for the answer (your message, recent chat history, the agent's character and any knowledge passages it uses) is sent through our server to Anthropic, which runs the AI model. We do not store these messages. Anthropic processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.
